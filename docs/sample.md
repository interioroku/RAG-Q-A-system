# Acme Corp Company Policy

Welcome to Acme Corp! This document outlines our core policies.

## Remote Work Policy
At Acme Corp, remote work is a core part of our culture. Employees are allowed to work from anywhere in the country, provided they have a stable internet connection and are online during the core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST.

## Training Budget
Every employee is allocated a annual learning and development budget of $2,000. This budget can be spent on books, online courses, conferences, or professional certifications that are relevant to their role.

## Communication Channels
- **Slack**: Used for daily real-time communication, team updates, and social chat.
- **Email**: Used for formal external communications and calendar invites.
- **Notion**: Used as our primary knowledge base and repository for team documentation.
